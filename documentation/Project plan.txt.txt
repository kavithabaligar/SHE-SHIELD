SHE-SHIELD
Inteligent women safety&journey assistance platform

PROBLEM:
Women may face safety concerns while travelling alone.
Especially in unifamiliar or isplated areas.

SOLUTION:
SHE-SHIELD is a web-based safety platform that helps users choose safer rootes,monitor their journeys,find nearby accessible places,and view community safety information.

MAIN MODULES:

1.safeRoute
-find routes
-calculate safety score
-Recommend a safer route

2.safejourney
-start a journey
-set expected arrival time
-confirm safe arrival
-trusted contact alert workflow

3.safePlace
-find nearby accessible places
-hospitals
-polie stations
-pharmacies
-public business/shops

4.safety map
-report safety concerns
-display reports on map
-generate area safety indicators

TECHNOLOGY:
Fronted:React.js
Backend:Node.js+Express.js
Database:MangoDB
Maps:Maps API
Version control:Git+Github
